## 1. The Short Version

Microsoft requires us to publish a privacy policy for Multi-Matter, even though we don't collect any data from our users through the application.

Fair enough, Microsoft. Here it is.

We don't collect your personal information. We don't track you. We don't spy on your devices. We don't sell your data. We don't even want your data!

Multi-Matter is designed to do one job: format your removable storage devices, en-masse, efficiently and safely.

Your devices, your files, your business.

## 2. Information We Collect (aka nothing)

Multi-Matter does not collect, transmit or upload any personal information, device information or usage data to its developer.

The application does not include:

- User accounts or registration requirements
- Analytics, tracking or telemetry systems
- Advertising or marketing trackers
- Background data uploads
- Cloud storage or remote data synchronisation
- Mechanisms for transmitting your files or device information to us

We receive absolutely zero information about who uses Multi-Matter, which devices they connect, what files they store or how frequently they use the software.

## 3. Information Processed Locally

To perform its intended functions, Multi-Matter accesses certain information provided by Windows about connected storage devices.

This may include:

- Drive letters and volume labels
- Storage capacity and device model
- Device and partition identifiers
- Removable-media classification and protection status
- Information required to validate and format selected volumes

This information is used locally to identify eligible removable devices, perform safety checks and execute formatting operations.

None of this information is ever transmitted to us unless you send it to us yourself (such as logs as part of a support request).

## 4. Local Settings and Diagnostic Files

Multi-Matter may create local application preferences and diagnostic records on your Windows computer.

These files are stored within:

"%LOCALAPPDATA%\Multi-Matter"

They may include preferences, drive-detection information, storage-operation details and application error messages.

These records exist to support application functionality and troubleshooting. They are not uploaded to us.

You may inspect or delete these files using Windows File Explorer. The application may recreate them during subsequent use.

## 5. Your Files and Storage Devices

Multi-Matter does not read, copy, upload or analyse the contents of your personal files for data-collection purposes (or for any purpose for that matter).

Its formatting functionality operates on removable storage volumes expressly selected by the user.

Formatting permanently erases existing data on those volumes. Users are responsible for checking their selections and maintaining appropriate backups before proceeding.

Formatting your USB drive is the application's job. Collecting what's on it is not.

## 6. Sharing, Selling and Disclosure of Information

Because Multi-Matter does not collect user or device information through the application, we have no application-generated user database to sell, share or disclose.

We do not sell personal information, provide user profiles to advertisers or share device-usage information with data brokers because *we don't get that data from you*.

No third-party analytics or advertising services are integrated into the application because we don't feel the need to pull ad revenue out of a tool that we made for the specific purpose of solving a problem for people for free.

## 7. Voluntary Support Communications

If you contact us by email, we will necessarily receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address, message and any attachments.

This information is used to respond to your enquiry, investigate reported issues and provide support.

Support correspondence may be processed and stored by our email service provider. We do not sell support correspondence or use it for unrelated marketing.

Please avoid sending passwords, confidential files or unnecessary personal information when requesting assistance.

You may contact us to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information provided through support communications, subject to any applicable legal retention requirements.

## 8. Data Storage, Retention and Security

Multi-Matter does not operate a remote database or cloud service containing application-generated user data.

Locally generated preferences and diagnostic files remain on your computer unless deleted by you or removed by another local process.

Their protection depends on the security of your Windows environment, including its file permissions and account protections.

We recommend keeping Windows updated and protecting your computer against unauthorised access.

We cannot guarantee the security of third-party services, operating systems or devices outside our control.

## 9. Microsoft Store and Third-Party Services

Multi-Matter may be distributed through the Microsoft Store or other authorised distribution channels at the sole discretion of the developer.

Microsoft may separately process information relating to Store downloads, purchases, installations or account activity under Microsoft's own privacy policies but we have absolutely zero control over that stuff.

Similarly, visiting a website that hosts this privacy policy may involve data processing by that website's hosting provider.

These activities are separate from Multi-Matter's operation and are governed by the relevant third party's policies.

We do not control their independent information-handling practices.

## 10. Children's Privacy

Multi-Matter does not knowingly collect personal information from children, not anyone else, through the application.

The software does not require users of any age to create an account or supply personal information to use its formatting functions. it's free to use for everyone!

## 11. International Data Transfers

Multi-Matter does not transmit application-generated personal information to our servers, whether in Australia or overseas. Heck, we don't even own servers! 

Information voluntarily provided through email support may be handled by service providers operating internationally, according to their applicable privacy policies and legal obligations.

## 12. Your Privacy Rights

You retain control of the information stored locally by Multi-Matter, subject to your operating system's permissions.

If you contact us for support, you may request information about any personal information we hold through that correspondence, or request its correction or deletion where applicable. The common sense answer to these requests will quickly identify that we don't have any of your personal information unless you've given it to us willingly. Even then, we aren't using it for anything other than helping you with your support request.

If hell freezes over, pigs become drones, and somehow we magically have personal data we aren't supposed to have, you can contact us with privacy questions or complaints. We will review them and respond within a reasonable period.

Nothing in this policy limits any rights you may have under applicable Australian privacy, consumer protection or other laws.

## 13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy if Multi-Matter's features, data-handling practices or legal obligations change.

Any updated policy will identify its latest revision date.

Should we ever introduce functionality that collects or transmits user information, this policy will be revised to explain that processing before the functionality is introduced.
